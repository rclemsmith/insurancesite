Plan Summary

Guaranteed wealth creation for conservative milestones

A dependable life insurance savings plan designed for people who want absolute certainty and zero surprises.

Unlike market-linked ULIPs or participating endowment plans whose future maturity value fluctuates with stock indices or annual company-declared bonuses, Kotak Assured Savings Plan delivers complete financial predictability. It is an individual, non-linked, non-participating life insurance savings plan where every rupee of your maturity benefit is calculated and locked in on the very day your policy commences.

Under this plan, you commit to paying premiums for a comfortable, limited term of 5, 6, 7, or 10 years, while your life cover and money continue working over a policy horizon of 10 to 20 years. Throughout your premium payment term, the policy credits Guaranteed Yearly Additions (GYA) at rates climbing as high as 10% of cumulative annualized premiums paid. At the end of the term, a lump sum Guaranteed Loyalty Addition (GLA) is paid in addition to your Basic Sum Assured.

Whether you are earmarking an untouchable higher-education fund for your child, preparing a wedding reserve, planning a debt payoff, or creating a risk-free safe bucket for retirement, Kotak Assured Savings Plan removes all guesswork. If life takes an unfortunate turn, your family is shielded by a comprehensive life cover that guarantees their financial stability.

Step-By-Step Journey

How Kotak Assured Savings Plan Works

A simple, disciplined journey from your first payment to guaranteed maturity payout.

  1. Choose Your Plan

    Decide how much you want to invest each year, pick a convenient premium payment term (5, 6, 7, or 10 years), and select a matching policy tenure (10 to 20 years) for your goal.

  2. Pay Premiums & Stay Covered

    Pay your chosen premium in yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, or monthly installments. Your comprehensive life insurance cover is active from day one, safeguarding your loved ones.

  3. Accumulate Guaranteed Additions

    Every year during your premium paying years, Guaranteed Yearly Additions (GYA) accrue between 7% and 10% on cumulative premiums paid. Zero market risk, zero volatility.

  4. Receive 100% Guaranteed Payout

    At maturity, collect your full maturity benefit: Basic Sum Assured + all accrued GYA + Guaranteed Loyalty Addition in a single lump sum, tax-free under prevailing laws.

Government Incentives

Tax Benefits on Kotak Assured Savings Plan

Enhance your effective returns with tax savings at both the investment and maturity stages.

Section 80C

Deduction on Premiums Paid

The premiums you pay towards Kotak Assured Savings Plan are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

  • Claim up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year against your taxable income.
  • Available under the Old Tax Regime for individual taxpayers and HUFs.
  • Premium must not exceed 10% of the sum assured to claim the full deduction.
Section 10(10D)

Tax-Free Maturity & Death Proceeds

Maturity and death claim proceeds enjoy attractive exemptions under Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

  • Death Benefit: 100% tax-exempt in the hands of the nominee under all circumstances, with zero caps.
  • Maturity Proceeds: 100% tax-free if the aggregate annual premium of your non-linked life insurance policies does not exceed ₹5,00,000 in any year.
  • Delivers pure, tax-shielded wealth directly into your hands at maturity.
Tax Note: Tax exemptions and deductions are subject to conditions prescribed under the Income Tax Act, 1961 and prevailing tax laws. Tax laws are subject to amendments from time to time. Please consult your chartered accountant or tax advisor for customized advice.
