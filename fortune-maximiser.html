A Wealth Accumulation Engine Built Around Your Life Goals

Kotak Fortune Maximiser combines long-term life cover with the flexibility to choose exactly how your bonus earnings are paid or reinvested.

Most traditional endowment plans lock you into a rigid structure where your payouts and timings are predetermined from day one. Kotak Fortune Maximiser takes a fundamentally different approach. As a limited-pay, participating endowment assurance plan, it participates in Kotak Life's with-profits fund, granting you three versatile bonus payout mechanisms: immediate cash, deferred cash accumulation, or compounding paid-up additions. This gives you absolute control over whether you receive cash flow today or compound your wealth for tomorrow.

Whether your goal is securing funds for your child's overseas education, creating a dedicated retirement transition engine, or building an intergenerational wealth reserve, the plan offers three dedicated variants: Life Goal Maximiser, Bright Future Maximiser, and Golden Years Maximiser. Each option is engineered for specific life chapters, offering tailored liquidity when you need it most.

In addition, Kotak Fortune Maximiser addresses family financial security holistically. It allows primary policyholders to add their spouse under the very same policy contract, avoiding duplicate policy administration costs. With coverage extending up to age 85, premium payment terms as short as 6 years, and enhanced benefits for female lives, this plan serves as a robust pillar for your family's financial roadmap.

Understanding the 3 Bonus Payout Mechanisms

1. Immediate Cash

Cash bonuses declared by Kotak Life are paid out directly to your bank account annually or monthly starting from the end of the first policy year.

2. Deferred Cash

Cash bonuses are accumulated and compounded with Kotak Life for a selected lock-in term (5, 7, or 10 years) before periodic payouts commence. Available under Life Goal Maximiser.

3. Paid-Up Additions (PUA)

Cash bonuses are utilised to purchase additional paid-up life insurance. These additions participate in future bonuses and compound until policy maturity or surrender.

How Kotak Fortune Maximiser Works

Tax Benefits Under Current Laws

Maximise your take-home income while growing long-term wealth under the provisions of the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961.

Section 80C Deduction

Premiums paid towards Kotak Fortune Maximiser qualify for tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh per financial year under Section 80C (under the Old Tax Regime), reducing your annual taxable income.

Section 10(10D) Exemption

Maturity and survival proceeds are tax-free under Section 10(10D), subject to the ₹5 Lakh aggregate annual premium limit for life insurance policies issued on or after April 1, 2023. Death benefit payouts to nominees are 100% tax-free with no upper limit.

*Tax laws are subject to amendments from time to time. Tax exemptions depend on your chosen tax regime and individual circumstances. Please consult your tax advisor for professional guidance.
