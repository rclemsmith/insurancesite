Plan Overview

A modern participating savings plan crafted for regular income flow and long-term legacy creation.

Kotak Get Assured Income Now (Kotak GAIN) is an individual, non-linked, participating life insurance savings plan designed for families and professionals seeking continuous liquidity without sacrificing long-term security. Unlike traditional endowment plans that lock your wealth until policy maturity, Kotak GAIN gives you the choice to receive regular income payouts as early as the end of the first policy month or first policy year.

The plan offers complete financial adaptability through three distinct plan options: receive early income for immediate recurring cash needs, let your payouts automatically purchase Paid-Up Additions (PUAs) to compound your wealth exponentially, or use Guaranteed Loyalty Additions to offset your future premium payments. Whatever your personal milestone, Kotak GAIN adapts smoothly to your life journey.

With convenient limited premium payment terms of 6, 8, 10, or 12 years, you only pay for a chosen short window while your comprehensive life cover safeguards your family up to age 85. In addition, the plan provides specially enhanced benefit structures for female lives, making it a powerful vehicle for women's independent financial planning and multi-generational wealth preservation.

Key Benefits

Comprehensive protection, guaranteed payouts, and participating bonus participation built into one plan.

Family Protection

Death Benefit

In the unfortunate event of the life insured's demise during the policy term, the nominee receives the highest of: (1) Sum Assured on Death (10x or 11x Annualised Premium based on entry age), (2) Sum Assured on Maturity, or (3) 105% of total premiums paid up to death. Plus, any accrued Paid-Up Additions, Interim Bonus, and Terminal Bonus declared are paid out in full, 100% tax-free under Section 10(10D).

Long-Term Wealth

Maturity Benefit

Upon surviving the complete policy term, you receive the Sum Assured on Maturity (calculated as Annualised Premium × PPT × Maturity Booster factor). In addition, you receive any accrued Guaranteed Loyalty Additions (Option C) or accumulated Paid-Up Additions (Option B), along with any declared Terminal Bonus, creating a substantial milestone corpus.

Regular Cash Flow

Survival Benefit (Option A)

Under Option A (Early Income), you enjoy regular income payouts comprising Guaranteed Income plus declared Cash Bonuses. These payouts start as early as the end of the 1st policy month (for monthly payouts) or 1st policy year (for annual payouts), providing predictable liquidity while keeping your full life cover intact.

Participating Growth

Bonus Structure

As a participating plan, Kotak GAIN allows you to share in the operational profits of Kotak Life's participating fund. Benefits may include regular Cash Bonuses (payable immediately or deferred), Interim Bonuses, and Terminal Bonuses at maturity or death, amplifying your total returns over the policy horizon.

Option A: Early Income

Immediate Liquidity & Continuous Cash Flow

Ideal for families seeking a dependable second income stream to finance recurring household expenses, child school fees, or lifestyle comforts without waiting years for payouts.

  • Guaranteed Income + declared Cash Bonus starts as early as Month 1 (monthly mode) or Year 1 (annual mode).

Option B: Paid-Up Additions

Long-Term Wealth Compounding

Designed for investors who want to maximize capital growth. Instead of withdrawing income, your declared benefits are automatically reinvested to purchase Paid-Up Additions (PUA).

  • Income benefits automatically buy additional paid-up life cover throughout the policy term.
  • Accrued PUAs compound over time and participate in future bonus declarations.
  • Flexibility to partially encash accrued PUAs for emergencies or receive as a lump sum at maturity or death.
  • Perfect for building a substantial college fund, wedding corpus, or retirement nest egg.

Option C: Premium Saver

Smart Savings with Premium Offsets

Crafted to ease your premium commitment in the latter years of the premium paying term, while accumulating a robust milestone corpus for future security.

  • Earn Guaranteed Loyalty Additions (GLAs) during the premium payment term.
  • GLAs can be utilized to help offset premium payments in latter years, easing financial burden.
  • Attractive Cash Bonuses kick in post-PPT to build long-term wealth for your family.
  • Ideal for individuals aiming to front-load savings and enjoy stress-free later years.

Tax Benefits

Save tax while building security and growing wealth under prevailing Indian tax laws.

Section 80C Deduction

Premiums paid towards Kotak GAIN are eligible for tax deductions of up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (for individuals opting for the Old Tax Regime). This reduces your taxable income every year you pay premiums.

Section 10(10D) Exemption

Maturity and survival proceeds received under the plan are completely exempt from income tax under Section 10(10D), subject to the aggregate annual premium limit of ₹5,00,000 across non-ULIP policies issued on or after 1st April 2023. In the event of death, all claim payouts to the nominee are 100% tax-free without any cap.

Please note: Tax benefits are subject to prevailing provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961 and amendments from time to time. Please consult your chartered accountant or tax advisor for advice tailored to your personal tax bracket.
