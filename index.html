Top wealth plans I recommend

Three Kotak Life savings plans that clients ask about most. GAIN and Fortune Maximiser are participating plans, so their bonuses are not guaranteed. Assured Savings Plan offers guaranteed benefits.

Kotak Get Assured Income Now (GAIN)

Limited pay, participating savings plan

A plan built around regular income for your family, with income benefits that can begin as early as the end of the first policy year.

  • Three plan options: Early Income, Paid-Up Addition and Premium Saver
  • Life cover until age 85
  • Optional riders for extra protection

Good fit if you want a steady income stream along with long-term cover.

Kotak Fortune Maximiser

Limited pay, participating endowment plan

A customisable plan for building a corpus for a goal, with a choice of how you receive your bonus.

  • Three plan options: Life Goal, Bright Future and Golden Years Maximiser
  • Bonus payout choice: immediate cash, deferred cash or paid-up additions
  • Life cover up to age 85, with optional riders

Good fit if you are saving for a goal such as education, a milestone or retirement.

Kotak Assured Savings Plan

Non-participating, non-linked savings plan

A simple plan for people who want to know their maturity amount upfront, with life cover while you save.

  • Guaranteed maturity benefit, plus guaranteed yearly additions and a guaranteed loyalty addition
  • Pay premiums for a shorter period: 5, 6, 7 or 10 years
  • Optional riders for term, accidental death and disability cover

Good fit if you prefer certainty over market or bonus-linked returns.
